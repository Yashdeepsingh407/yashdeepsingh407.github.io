About me

I'm Yashdeep, but everyone calls me Yash. I live in Berlin, study Digital Business and work at a water-tech startup.

Professionally lazy.

I grew up in Germany and speak German, English, Hindi and Punjabi. I study Digital Business at BHT Berlin and work as a working student in business development and marketing at Hydro-Berlin, a Berlin startup for groundwater monitoring.

My motto is a quote often attributed to Bill Gates: choose a lazy person for a hard job, because they will find an easy way to do it. Nobody has found where he actually said it, but I like the idea. It is why I test AI tools, keep what works and write it down.

I started with websites in 2020, then ran ads and the online shop of our family store. In 2022 I took part in Jugend forscht with a study on water use near the Tesla factory and won a special award. Water has been my topic since.

Languages: German C2 · English fluent · Hindi · Punjabi
