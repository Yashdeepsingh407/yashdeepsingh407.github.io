RoboPay

Robots are starting to deliver our parcels. But a robot can't open a bank account. So how does it get paid?

Role
Business and market research
Stack
Solana, peaq, Staex
Since
June 2026

In one sentence

RoboPay is payments for the machine economy: the robot proves it delivered, and the money moves by itself.

What you see in the video

Our working prototype, a small PiCar-X robot car, doing a real delivery.

OrderA customer orders a delivery and pays 0.2 SOL
EscrowThe money doesn't go to the robot yet. It's locked in an escrow program on Solana
DeliverThe car drives to the drop-off and scans the QR code with its camera
SignThat confirmation is signed on-chain
ReleaseThe escrow pays the car's owner directly. No middleman

Every release lands in the transaction history, and anyone can check it on the Solana Explorer. Our letterbox prototype even opens by itself.

Three layers make it work

ConnectivityStaex

Keeps the robot securely connected over 4G.

Identitypeaq

Gives the robot a verifiable identity: a machine NFT that proves who owns it.

PaymentsSolana

Moves the money, from escrow to owner.

The vision

It's not just cars. The same stack works for delivery drones, boats and humanoid robots. Any machine that delivers can get paid.
