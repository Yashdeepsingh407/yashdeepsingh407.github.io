RoboPay

Payment rails for robots. A delivery robot proves it delivered and releases the payment itself, without a person approving it.

Role
Business and market research
Stack
Solana escrow, GPS, QR, AI vision
Since
June 2026

The problem

Robots do the work. A person still has to approve the payment.

How it works

LockThe payment is locked in an escrow smart contract on Solana
DeliverThe robot drives to the customer
ProveGPS geofencing, a QR scan at handover, or an AI agent checking a photo
ReleaseWith valid proof, the contract releases the payment on its own

My part

Business and market research for the paper. The build is by Kevin Ehrentraut and Xinyan Liu.

Supervised by Prof. Dr. Alexandra K. Mikityuk, supported by Staex.
