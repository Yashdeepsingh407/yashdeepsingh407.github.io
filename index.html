10.5 m Research

RoboPay

HTW Berlin, Machine Economy Lab, Jun 2026 to today

The flagship project of the Machine Economy Lab, a registered research project running 2026 to 2029. We're building a trustless payment layer for autonomous machines: robots, drones and delivery vehicles confirm their own work and release payment from a Solana escrow smart contract. No human in the loop.

Proof of delivery comes from GPS geofencing, a QR code scan at handover, or an AI agent that checks the delivery visually.

With Kevin Ehrentraut and Xinyan Liu, supervised by Prof. Dr. Alexandra K. Mikityuk.

  1. Payment locked in escrow
  2. Robot drives to the customer
  3. Location inside the geofence
  4. Escrow releases payment on Solana
