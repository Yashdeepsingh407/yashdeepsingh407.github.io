7 Claude prompts that make studying faster

Stop re-reading. Use Claude as a patient tutor that tests you instead.

These are the prompts I reach for when I study for exams in my Digital Business degree. Replace the words in [brackets] and paste.

1. Explain it from zero

Explain [topic] to me as if I were a first-year student. Use one everyday example. Then ask me 3 questions to check that I understood it.

2. Quiz me

Quiz me on [topic], one question at a time. Wait for my answer, tell me what is right or wrong, explain briefly, then ask the next question. Start easy and get harder.

3. One-page cheat sheet

Here are my lecture notes: [paste notes]. Turn them into a one-page cheat sheet with headings, key terms and formulas. Keep it under 300 words.

4. Find the gaps

Here are my notes on [topic]: [paste notes]. What important points are missing or unclear? List them, most important first.

5. Explain it back

I will explain [topic] in my own words. Point out everything that is wrong or vague, and tell me how to say it more precisely.

6. Exam plan

My exam is in [number] days. Topics: [list]. I can study [hours] hours per day. Make a day-by-day plan with 90-minute blocks and one short review block each day.

7. Flashcards

Turn these notes into 20 flashcards in the format Question | Answer: [paste notes]

Two rules

  • Check facts. Claude can be wrong. For anything graded, compare with your lecture slides or the textbook.
  • Do the work yourself. The point is to test yourself, not to let the AI answer for you.
