RoboPay in 90 seconds: how a robot pays for itself

A robot needs electricity and parts. RoboPay lets it pay for them on its own.

SolanaAgent5 min read

The flow

  1. Robot needs a service (charging, repair, data).
  2. It requests a price from the provider.
  3. It pays with a small blockchain transaction on Solana.
  4. Service starts as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Why Solana

Payments settle in seconds and cost a fraction of a cent, which is what you need when machines make many small payments.
