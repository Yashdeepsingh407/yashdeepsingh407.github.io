Set up a Claude Project for studying or job hunting

Stop pasting the same background into every chat.

A Claude Project keeps related chats together with shared instructions and files. That means you explain your situation once.

  1. Create a project with a clear name, like "Job search 2026".
  2. Write the instructions once. Who you are, what you want, how Claude should answer.
  3. Add files. Your CV, the job ads, your lecture notes.
  4. Start chats inside it. Every new chat already knows the background.

Starter instructions

I am a student looking for a working-student role in business development.
Answer in a friendly, direct tone. Keep answers short unless I ask for detail.
Never invent experience that is not in my CV. If something is missing, ask me.
Always tell me when you are unsure.

Keep it private

Do not upload documents that contain other people's personal data or anything you are not allowed to share.
