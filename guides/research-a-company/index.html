Research any company in 10 minutes with Claude

Before an interview or a sales call, get a briefing that cites its sources.

Use a Claude chat that can search the web, so the answers are current. Ask for sources every time.

The prompt

Research [company name] and write a one-page briefing:
- What they do and who their customers are
- Their latest news from the last 6 months
- Their main competitors
- 3 smart questions I could ask in an interview or call

For every claim add the source link. If you cannot find something, say "not found". Do not guess.

Check it in 3 minutes

  1. Open two links. Click at least two sources and confirm they say what Claude claims.
  2. Check the date. Old news is the most common error.
  3. Mark the gaps. Everything marked "not found" is a question for the conversation.
